# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-MMMM
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation, constraint or question forced a decision. Facts, not
opinions; link to issues, measurements or upstream documentation.

## Decision

The decision in one or two sentences, then the concrete shape it takes in
the code or configuration.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what is deliberately out of scope,
and what would make us revisit the decision.
